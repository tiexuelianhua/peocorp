About Peo Corp

PEO CORP: OUR MISSION

Here at Peo Corp, our sole mission is to follow Peo, the one who found the Peoverse and inspired all of us. In doing so, we aim to support our very home, the Peoverse, and bring about a better tomorrow.

We base our work on the core tradition of North Peoville: the expedition. With our ever-growing, ever-expanding team, we hope to pave the way towards new frontiers, expediting towards infinity.

PEO CORP: BACKGROUND

Hello agents! Here’s a small background on Peo Corp!

Our corporation follows in Peo’s footsteps. We travel to other universes to extract energy. Just how do we do this? I’ll explain right now!

Within our facilities, we contain hensa and creatures native to the universes we enter (including our own, if necessary)!
(Note that hensa are anomalies not originating in the current reality, wherever that may be! The term was coined by our friends at the K.S.I. - the Kipshyrian Supernatural Investigators organization).

Through the entities we contain, we are able to generate essences through a complex and closely-guarded process. To not bore you, just know that containment = energy!

There are 6 different branches/teams in Peo Corp. Here is a list of all of them:

  1. The Hilltop Branch (uppermost branch)
  2. The Realmouth Branch
  3. The Dreamland Branch
  4. The Kipshyrian Branch
  5. The Zomgoosian Branch
  6. The Southern Branch (lowermost branch)

Each team has a captain, who is in charge of all the affairs in their branch. Teams closer to the bottom typically contain more dangerous hensa, while teams near the top often contain local universe entities.

Who’s the manager of all these branches? Me, of course! However, I’m only the manager of this facility. There are other Peo Corp facilities in other universes, but I’m happy to announce that I manage the main Peo Corp facility!

Thank you for taking the time to read this. I hope you find your time at Peo Corp worthwhile! May Peo be with you.

Sincerely,
Peo Corp Manager

PEO CORP: OUR REVISED MISSION

You can keep following footsteps, but what if they suddenly stop? What if the path forward was unclear? Did the person here before you backtrack? Did they take another path that suddenly got covered? Or, incredibly unlikely, but did they straight up vanish?

What do you do next?

Would you continue where the footsteps ended, attempting to salvage all your progress so far despite the uncertainty? Would you stop and take a break before thinking about your next course of action, or would you also backtrack and simply return to where you came from?

I’m sure we would have kept going, salvaging what we had accomplished up until now.

But that wasn’t possible. Not with the sheer amount of losses we sustained. Enough is enough, even for us. Even for what we did.

Maybe we don’t deserve to determine what’s enough. Maybe our sins were still far from forgiven.

Whatever the answer, we backtracked, coming back to where we started. We didn’t have any other choice.

They chased us endlessly. It was terrifying. Even up to this day, I can’t wrap my head around it.

Everyone I knew and cared about, everyone I’ve gotten to know over these months, put to the slaughter as if they were worthless.

I’m not unique in that regard, either. There are many like me. At this point, could any of us muster the courage to keep walking along the path of uncertainty?

We’re no longer following footsteps. We’ve decided it’s best to return to the comfort of home, a home that’s been increasingly at risk of being destroyed since we began our mission.

The results of our mission are a lesson. That much, I’ve learned.

I can’t say I know how to keep going. I’m just one agent, one survivor, out of many. I’m sure others feel the same, too.

Our original mission is no longer relevant. Peo Corp, as a whole, has crumbled. We’re trying our best to make do with the remnants.

What is our new mission, then?

It is to understand.

That at the end of the path, no, at the very start.

That the footsteps we follow shouldn’t be ones that trample without care. That the path we choose to take is one that allows the flowers to bloom at its side.

That the path we continue on nurtures those flowers into lush, bountiful fields where the butterflies may harvest, forevermore.

That whoever tends to those flowers can honor the path we laid before them.

That in expediting towards infinity, we can be understood, too.
