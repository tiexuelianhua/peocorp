Dreamland Branch

DREAMLAND BRANCH: BACKGROUND

The Dreamland Branch, the third branch of Peo Corp, is the one that is most focused on the development of pharmaceuticals, and, in stark contrast, scouting out new Southern areas for the Zomgoosian Branch.

The main quirk of Dreamland agents is their masks; every Dreamland agent has their own personal mask, each with the characteristic expression and only personalized at the very edges. The mask serves as a literal filter for whatever they may encounter in the South, whether it be worsened vision, hallucinatory experiences, abnormal sounds, or more.

Why don’t other branches (besides the Southern Branch) have this kind of gear? Simple - it just isn’t their job to trailblaze the undiscovered South. The only reason the Southern Branch gets similar features on their armor is due to the kind of threats they face as well as their primary role. Besides that, the masks are a Dreamland Branch-exclusive.

Hensa contained here are somewhat similar to those in the Realmouth Branch in the sense that they do not pose significant threats, as any unpredictable variables could be disastrous, especially in the field of pharmaceuticals. Research done with hensa in the Dreamland Branch goes towards enhancing the normal pill, amongst other things.

As of the END OF PEO CORP, there was a considerable drop in the number of Dreamland Branch facilities, though the branch itself is still functional as a considerable number of facilities are located in North Peoville. Still, our hearts go out to those who perished for our cause.
