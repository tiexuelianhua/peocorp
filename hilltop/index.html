Hilltop Branch

HILLTOP BRANCH: BACKGROUND

The Hilltop Branch is the first and uppermost branch of Peo Corp, dealing with everyday affairs in North Peoville, handling weaker strays from the South and containing hensa that may potentially enhance life in the Peoverse (on top of providing energy).

In each main room of a Hilltop Branch facility lies a sizable hilltop that almost reaches the ceiling. On top of it lies the captain’s station. The hilltop is also this branch’s gimmick; it doubles as a place for meetings and a haven of refuge in case of containment breaches. Some rumors say the hilltop has another function that’s exclusively reserved for situations even I’m unaware of, though the function is incomplete.

Do not be fooled by this branch’s placement; it is, without a doubt, able to stand its ground against threats much stronger than the hensa it contains. The only reason it contains the least dangerous hensa is because multiple Hilltop Branches reside within North Peoville.

As of the END OF PEO CORP, most Hilltop Branches have been eradicated, with the only remaining ones being located in the Peoverse. Our hearts go out to those who perished for our cause.
