Branch

Description

KP-01-05, otherwise known as Jimbob, is an enchanted mace. In terms of appearance, there is nothing abnormal; however, in regards to its properties, Jimbob is special. Its enchantments are at impossible levels, infinitely surpassing those normally obtainable.

Ability

Due to its properties, anyone wielding Jimbob is capable of instantly killing nearly any living being. Limited tests to gauge its strength have been conducted; these tests are detailed in the Addendums below.

Origin

Jimbob is said to have originated from a mysterious underground temple during one of the phases of the Blair SMP. The temple and hensa themselves were first discovered by HT-Agent Jam through temporary reality-bending means.

The story behind the temple and Jimbob is mostly unknown. As of now, we’ve concluded that it just happened to be in the Blair SMP.

Details

Jimbob is absolutely off limits to any agent, regardless of the branch. Work is conducted from afar using Kipshyrian technology in order to examine its properties and attempt to enhance current weaponry.

Escape Information

Jimbob must not be wielded by anyone for 20 minutes 5 minutes 30 seconds any period of time. Refer to the Addendums as to why. Besides that, Jimbob itself cannot escape on its own, as it is just a mace.
