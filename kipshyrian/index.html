Kipshyrian Branch

KIPSHYRIAN BRANCH: BACKGROUND

The Kipshyrian Branch, the fourth branch of Peo Corp, is the branch focused on manufacturing and building. This branch is responsible for building new facilities for all branches, as well as producing most tools, weapons, and machinery, very much akin to its namesake, the Kipshyrian Archipelago.

The gimmick of the Kipshyrian Branch is a signature tool each agent carries with them. The tool is a pocket-sized cube, engraved with orange runes reminiscent of the Kipshyrian Archipelago’s iconography. With these, agents are able to quickly construct objects, with their size depending on how much energy is expended. Of course, we’d like to be as efficient as possible in energy extraction, so each agent has a separate energy container linked to their cube so that they don’t disrupt our ability to meet daily quotas.

Hensa contained in this branch are both numerous and formidable due to the capabilities of Kipshyrian agents. Kipshyrian Branches produce a great deal of energy for this reason. The research done here is for the primary purpose of developing gear or tools from hensa.

As of the END OF PEO CORP, the Kipshyrian Branch suffered a crippling loss in number, with the only active facilities being located in the Kipshyrian Archipelago. Our hearts go out to those who perished for our cause.
