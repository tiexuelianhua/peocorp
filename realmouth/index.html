Realmouth Branch

REALMOUTH BRANCH: BACKGROUND

The Realmouth Branch, the second branch of Peo Corp, also plays a role in North Peoville affairs; however, they are more focused on refining and developing rituals as well as containing hensa that may facilitate these processes in any way.

Within the main room of each Realmouth Branch facility is a round table without anything on it. On this table are numerous inscribed patterns. Anyone who sits at this table receives full saturation and an overall boost in energy.

Like most other branches of Peo Corp, the Realmouth Branch is able to consistently punch above its weight. Regardless, it is a branch focused on research, both of rituals and of hensa.

A lot of the hensa contained here are ones that have been moved from the Southern Branch. As you can imagine, incorporating hensa into rituals can potentially spell the end of Peo Corp itself, so precautions and strict preparations are taken during a hensa’s containment in the Southern Branch to ensure disaster doesn’t befall Peo Corp. Hensa are only moved after they are deemed non-threatening and safe to incorporate into research in the Realmouth Branch.

As the main branch behind Peo Corp’s rituals, the Realmouth Branch is responsible for particular protocols such as CODE-PRESERVE, developing the initial requirements and method to link candles to agents. Their aforementioned gimmick, the table’s abilities in the main room, is another CODE called CODE-SUSTAIN, though you can imagine its consequences are drastically different.

As of the END OF PEO CORP, a wide majority of Realmouth Branches were eradicated, with the remaining facilities primarily being located in the Peoverse. Our hearts go out to those who perished for our cause.
