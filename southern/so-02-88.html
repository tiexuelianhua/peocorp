Branch
Southern Branch

Description

SO-02-88 is a book covered in sculk, incredibly similar to the journals obtained during the ███████████████ (hence the 02 in its assigned code). It has undergone similar purification rituals to the other books, but has a unique page that cannot be purified. Thus, it is only read in controlled environments by people who have been explicitly given clearance.

Apart from the page, there is nothing else particularly special about SO-02-88 in terms of physical appearance and construction.

Ability

Like any sculk-infested object obtained from the ███████████████, it has been thought that SO-02-88 is capable of assimilating anything nearby into more sculk, though it has never been put to the test as a precaution.

The main thing that differentiates this journal from any of the other journals obtained from that era are its contents, which are rather foreign and unusual, especially when compared to all the other acquired journals during this event.

Origin

The journal was dated to have originated from the time of the ███████████████.

For the most part, the contents suggest that this journal was written by someone who was mostly untouched by the incident in that time period. The constantly infested page, however, is of an unknown origin. We theorize that the original writer’s place of residence was overrun by sculk at some point in time, but we do not know why the sculk on the page won’t be purified.

Details

Nothing of note, as the purifiers take care of the job. The reading of its contents is limited only to agents granted clearance by the higher ups. As with any foreign journal reading, it is to be done from afar through the use of unit mechanisms.
