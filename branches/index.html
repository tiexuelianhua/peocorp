PEO CORP: BACKGROUND

Hello agents! Today I will explain to you what Peo Corp is!

Our corporation follows in Peo’s footsteps. We travel to other universes to extract energy. Just how do we do this? I’ll explain right now!

Within our facilities, we contain hensa and creatures native to the universes we enter!
(Note that hensa are anomalies not originating in the current reality, wherever that may be! The term was coined by our friends at the K.S.I. - the Kipshyrian Supernatural Investigators organization)

Through the entities we contain, we are able to generate essences through a complex and closely-guarded process. To not bore you, just know that containment = energy!

There are 6 different branches/teams in Peo Corp. Here is a list of all of them:

  1. The Hilltop Branch (uppermost branch)
  2. The Realmouth Branch
  3. The Dreamland Branch
  4. The Kipshyrian Branch
  5. The Zomgoosian Branch
  6. The Southern Branch (lowermost branch)

Each team has a captain, who is in charge of all the affairs in their branch. Teams closer to the bottom typically contain more dangerous hensa, while teams near the top often contain local universe entities.

Who’s the manager of all these branches? Me, of course! However, I’m only the manager of this facility. There are other Peo Corp facilities in other universes, but I’m happy to announce that we are the main Peo Corp facility!

Thank you for taking the time to read this. I hope you find your time at Peo Corp worthwhile. May Peo be with you.

Sincerely,
Peo Corp Manager
