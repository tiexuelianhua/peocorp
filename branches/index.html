Branches

PEO CORP: BACKGROUND

  • Hilltop Branch

    Uppermost

    The Hilltop Branch is the first and uppermost branch of Peo Corp, dealing with everyday affairs in North Peoville.

  • Realmouth Branch

    The Realmouth Branch is the second branch of Peo Corp, focused on refining and developing rituals.

  • Dreamland Branch

    The Dreamland Branch is the third branch of Peo Corp, focused on developing pharmaceuticals and scouting new Southern areas.

  • Kipshyrian Branch

    The Kipshyrian Branch is the fourth branch of Peo Corp, focused on manufacturing and building.

  • Zomgoosian Branch

    The Zomgoosian Branch is the fifth branch of Peo Corp, focused on expeditions to the South.

  • Southern Branch

    Lowermost

    The Southern Branch is the sixth and lowermost branch of Peo Corp, being the absolute strongest in terms of firepower and ability.
