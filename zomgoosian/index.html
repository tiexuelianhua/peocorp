Zomgoosian Branch

ZOMGOOSIAN BRANCH: BACKGROUND

The Zomgoosian Branch, the fifth branch of Peo Corp, is the primary branch involved in expeditions to the South. Besides typical Peoverse expeditions, this branch is responsible for frequent, often unheard of expeditions to continue gaining traction against the ever-growing threat of the South.

The Zomgoosian Branch, Dreamland Branch, and Southern Branch all work in tandem in regards to the South. If the Dreamland Branch trailblazes, the Zomgoosian Branch establishes said trail and sets camp, while the Southern Branch holds the camp’s grounds if ever necessary.

The gimmick of the Zomgoosian Branch is rather interesting. Each agent has a rather sizable camera, akin to a professional one, that has Zomgoose’s face imprinted on the lens. They are a sort of sidegrade to the characteristic mask of the Dreamland Branch; after using these cameras, the shot neutralizes any detrimental effects in the immediate front-facing vicinity of the user. Unfortunately, they don’t actually take pictures.

Hensa contained in the Zomgoosian Branch are things that derive from the South, as the branch’s main goal is to adapt to the South’s forces. Research is conducted frequently on these hensa, both to evaluate their nature and to develop more gear specifically effective against the South. The Zomgoosian Branch may also contain native Southern entities, though that is more uncommon as they do not last long outside of the South.

As of the END OF PEO CORP, many Zomgoosian Branch facilities were quickly wiped, as if something held a grudge against them. May Peo be with those fallen souls that gave up their lives for our cause.
